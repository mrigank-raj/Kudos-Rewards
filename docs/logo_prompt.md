**Instructions:** Copy and paste these prompts into Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or your preferred AI image generator. The prompts are optimized to match the modern, corporate, and tech-forward aesthetic described in your UI redesign document.

### Tips for generating the perfect logo:
1. **No Text (`--no text`):** AI struggles with spelling. It's usually best to generate just the icon/mark, and then add the word "Kudos" next to it using a clean font (like *Inter* or *Plus Jakarta Sans*) in Figma or Photoshop.
2. **Variants:** If you use Midjourney, append `--v 6.0` to the end of the prompts for the most modern, highest-quality vector styles.
3. **Colors:** The prompts use indigo, purple, and cyan. Feel free to swap these out in the prompt with your chosen primary brand colors.
